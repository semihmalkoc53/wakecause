Help with WakeCause: Sleep Tracker

Contact us: open a request at github.com/semihmalkoc53/wakecause/issues. We usually reply within two business days.

Tracking does not pick up sounds

  • Allow microphone access in iOS Settings → WakeCause.
  • Place the iPhone on the nightstand with the microphone facing you, plugged in.
  • While tracking, the "Hearing" indicator shows what the app hears. A sound is added to the list a few seconds after it ends.

My report says "Estimated from sound and motion"

Without Apple Watch sleep data the app estimates awakenings. If you wear an Apple Watch, connect Apple Health in Settings and tap "Re-analyse with Apple Watch data" a few minutes after you wake.

Change the language

Open Settings → Language in the app.

Subscriptions

Manage or cancel in iOS Settings → your name → Subscriptions. To restore a purchase on a new device, open the Premium screen and tap "Restore purchases".

Delete my data

Swipe to delete a night in the Nights tab, or delete the app to remove everything. WakeCause keeps no data on servers.
