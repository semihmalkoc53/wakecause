Privacy Policy

Effective date: October 30, 2026

This policy explains how WakeCause: Sleep Tracker ("WakeCause", "the app"), published by Semih Malkoç, handles your information. In short: the app does not collect, upload, sell or share your data. Everything stays on your device.

Microphone and audio

While you track a night, WakeCause uses the microphone to analyse sounds on your iPhone with Apple's on-device sound classification. Raw audio is never stored or uploaded. To let you listen back, the app keeps short clips (about 15 seconds) around detected sounds. These clips are stored only on your device and are deleted when you delete the night or the app.

Apple Health (HealthKit)

If you allow it, WakeCause reads sleep stages and heart rate from Apple Health and writes the time you spent in bed. Health data is processed on your device to build your sleep report. It is never sent to us or to third parties, never used for advertising or marketing, and never stored in iCloud by the app.

Motion

The app may read your iPhone's accelerometer during a tracked night to notice movement. This data stays on your device.

Purchases

Subscriptions are processed by Apple through the App Store. We do not receive or store your payment details. Apple provides us with anonymous, aggregated sales reports only.

No accounts, analytics or tracking

WakeCause has no user accounts, does not use third-party analytics or advertising SDKs, and does not track you across apps or websites.

Children

The app is not directed at children under 13 and does not knowingly collect information from them.

Your choices

  • Turn off microphone or Health access at any time in iOS Settings.
  • Delete individual nights in the app, or delete the app to remove all of its data.

Not a medical device

WakeCause is intended for general wellness only. It does not diagnose, treat or prevent any medical condition.

Changes

If this policy changes, the new version will be published on this page with an updated effective date.

Contact

Questions about privacy: see the support page.
