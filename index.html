WakeCause: Sleep Tracker

Find out what wakes you.

WakeCause listens through the night on your iPhone, recognises sounds such as snoring, coughing, barking, a crying baby or traffic, and links them to the moments you woke up. With Apple Watch it uses your sleep stages; without one it estimates from sound and motion.

Audio is analysed on your device. Nothing is uploaded.
